The Karjanian Secondary League (KSL) (Routan: *Karjanmaan Kakkosliiga*) is a professional football league in Karjania and the second highest level of the Karjanian Football pyramid. It is contested by 10 teams over 18 Matchdays, and the top two finishers are promoted to the [[karjanian-premier-league]], while the 10th placed team is relegated to the Karjanian Kolmonen. It was founded in 1909 and has been contested every year since, with the exception of years during [[great-war|Great War]].

## Structure

The KSL is played from early September to late May each year, with 18 total Matchdays. Each of the 10 teams play each other twice, meaning 9 home and 9 away games for each of the 10 teams. A win gives 3 points, a draw 1, and a loss 0. At the end of the season, the two highest finishing teams are promoted to the KPL, and are replaced by the two lowest finishing teams from the first division. The 10th finisher of the KSL is relegated to the Karjanian Kolmonen, the bottom of the Karjanian football pyramid, and is replaced by the winner of the Kolmonen. Teams from the KSL compete in the Karjanian Cup, and have won it in the past. The record for most points under the current format is held by Tsirvakka JK in the 1927 season with 42 points.

## History

After the foundation of the KPL in 1896, the teams in Karjania who weren't accepted in would go on to continue playing in regional tournaments. Tsirvakka JK, Åulot FK and Kuittijärvi JK all won the Karjanian Cup despite not being able to compete for a spot in the first division. In 1908, the Karjanian Football Association decided to expand the KPL to 12 teams, and for the following year introduce a Promotion-Relegation system. The inaugural Karjanian Secondary League in 1909 had 12 teams compete over 22 matchdays and was won by Surtuja JK over Åulot FK, with both gaining promotion to the KPL for 1910. Arkankeli FK won the 1911 season, before Syväsjärvi JK set an all time points record with 54 points in 22 games following their shock final day relegation from the KPL in 1912. The 1913 season ended with XIV. District JK and Murmánska Partisani finishing 1st and 2nd, and this is regarded as the greatest season in second division history, with 2 former KPL champions competing. KHY Palloseura and Vostamo JK were promoted the season after, and the 1914 edition was cut short by the Great War. It was officially awarded to Tsirvakka JK, with cup winners Kalaraava JK also gaining promotion to the first division. KHY Palloseura won the first season after the war, and JK Pietari won the 1919 season before the Karjanian Civil War changed Karjanian football forever. The current format was introduced for the 1921 season, which was won by Åulot FK. Surtuja JK won in 1922 and Perona JS in 1923. Musta Roudanjärvi took promotion in 1924 along with Narovsky JK. Kämyrvi FK and Kalaraava JK were promoted the year after. Laatokka JK won the 1926 season by 1 point over Arkankeli FK, who they defeated 4-3 on the final matchday. Tsirvakka JK set the point record for the current format with 42 in 1927, getting promoted alongside Kuittijärvi JK. The 1929 season was won by Goal difference by Surtuja JK over Rautaniemi JK who made their first division debut next year. With 30 points, Surtuja JK set a record low under the current format. Surtuja JK won their 4th title in 1931, making them the record winners of the KSL. Arkankeli FK and Åulot FK were promoted in 1932. The 1933 season gave Kalaraava JK it's first title, winning by goal difference on the final day over Tolvajärvi PS, who were promoted to the first division for the first time in their history.
