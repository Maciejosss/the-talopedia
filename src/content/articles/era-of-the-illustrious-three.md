The **Era of the Illustrious Three** (Skjarnish: *De Tre Lysande Gestalternas Era*) is a period in [[skjarnland|Skjarnish]] history named for its three monarchs; [[johan-ii|Johan II]], [[vilhelm-i]] and [[karl-iv|Karl IV]]. The era saw the creation of the [[kingdom-of-skjarnland]] following the [[wars-of-skjarnish-unification|unification of the Skjarnish realms]] under Johan II of [[sundberg-union|Skjarnia and Norrvynia]], the adoption of [Veritism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism) during the [[reformation]], the [[age-of-consolidation|consolidation of executive power]] under the [[house-of-steinfelt|Steinfelt monarchy]], and the transformation of the government and military to sustain Skjarnland's growing military and political ambition, which became one of the largest and most organised bureaucracies in Evria by the dawn of the 17th century.

The era was defined by centralisation of power under the King of Skjarnland, the weakening of the noble class and the gradual phasing out of feudalism. The development of the Skjarnish state and military would lay the foundations of its [[skjarnish-great-power-era|Great Power era]], considered by Skjarnish historians to have begun following the coronation of [[gustaf-i-fridolf]] in March 1601.

## Early Rule of Johan II

Johan II ascended to the throne of the Sundberg Union (the personal union between [[kingdom-of-skjarnia|Skjarnia]] and [[kingdom-of-norrvynia|Norrvynia]] lasting since 1272) on 13 August 1498, succeeding his father [[karl-iii|Karl III]] following his death in June and marking the end of the [[age-of-tyranny]], which saw the Crown become reliant on several competing aristocratic families to govern its territories, collect revenues, and raise troops.

By the time of Johan's coronation, the nobility had become a powerful political force capable of challenging the King under certain situations when unified, but suffered from longstanding rivalries and a reputation among the people for being oppressive. In reality, this image stems from the peak of the Tyranny in the 1470s, with the worst incidents including the [[trosso-bloodbath|Trossö Bloodbath]] perpetrated by a limited amount of nobles. This reputation nevertheless remained strong.

The Sundberg Union remained the dominant trading power in the Suebian, competing with its principal rival [[kingdom-of-danyria|Danyria]], controlled by the [[house-of-hva|Hva dynasty]], which was allied to several states in the [[holy-evrian-empire]] including [[duchy-of-brimund|Brimund]] and [[nordmark]], whose main motivations were to counter the Union's power in Northern Evria. Tensions had remained high since the brief [[war-of-the-strait]] in 1484, which saw Union forces successfully land in [[jylland]], but later suffer defeat and explusion from the peninsula by a combined Brimund-Danyrian force using Arvernois-inspired [troisième](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tercio) heavy infantry formations, which was notoriously difficult to dislodge using conventional tactics.

Consequently, Johan began funding military development led by [[olof-larsson|Count Olof Larsson]] to counter or adopt the troisième, learning from the lessons of the War of the Strait and observing several conflicts in Evria from 1500-1510, including the [[turulian-wars]], inspired by Turulian forces' use of light infantry units to outmaneuver the heavier and slower Arlbernian formations and inflict losses. Meanwhile in [[stralholm|Strålholm]], Johan undertook several measures to increase royal power, including the cultivation of loyal administrators from several families with good relations with the Crown, the passage of several laws to enable him to collect taxes directly, and the reduction of military dependence on noble houses.

In 1512, the [[vistgarde-incident]], a brief clash between Strålholmer traders and Danyrian merchants of the [[havnia-league|Hävnia League]] over a commercial dispute, escalated into a wider diplomatic crisis. Danyria and the Holy Evrian Empire began a trade boycott against merchants from Skjarnia, while excluding Norrvynians from the new restrictions in an effort to entice division within the Sundberg Union. The boycott severely disrupted Skjarnian commerce, resulting in a fiscal crisis. Despite concessions offered by Johan, including monetary reparations for killed Danyrians, [[frederik-iv|Frederik IV]] refused to end the boycott, viewing it as an effective means of eroding rival trade influence without resorting to war.

After several years of unsuccessful negotiations, Johan convened his war council in 1516 to prepare for war against Danyria and its allies within the Holy Evrian Empire, including Brimund, with the goal of restoring Skjarnian access to restricted Suebian ports, the reduction of Danyrian commercial influence in the region, and establish crucial control over the [[hylmstrond|Hlymrströnd]], the strait separating Skjarnia and Danyria. Through war, Johan hoped to remove one of the Sundberg Union's greatest strategic vulnerabilities.
