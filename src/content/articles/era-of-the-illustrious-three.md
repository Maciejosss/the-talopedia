The **Era of the Illustrious Three** (Skjarnish: *De Tre Lysande Gestalternas Era*) is a period in [[skjarnland|Skjarnish]] history named for its three monarchs; [[johan-ii|Johan II]], [[vilhelm-i]] and [[karl-iv|Karl IV]]. The era saw the creation of the Kingdom of Skjarnland following the [[wars-of-skjarnish-unification|unification of the Skjarnish realms]] under Johan II, the adoption of [Veritism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism) during the [[reformation]], the [[age-of-consolidation|consolidation of executive power]] under the [[house-of-steinfelt|Steinfelt monarchy]], and the transformation of the government and military to sustain Skjarnland's growing military and political ambition, which became one of the largest and most organised bureaucracies in Evria by the dawn of the 17th century.

The era was defined by centralisation of power under the King of Skjarnland, the weakening of the noble class and the fading of feudalism. The development of the Skjarnish state and military would lay the foundations of its [[skjarnish-great-power-era|Great Power era]], considered by Skjarnish historians to have begun following the coronation of [[gustaf-i-fridolf]] in March 1601.

## Wars of Skjarnish Unification

Johan II ascended to the Skjarnian throne on 13 August 1498, succeeding his father Karl I following his death in June and ending the [[age-of-tyranny]].
