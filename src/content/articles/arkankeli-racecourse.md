Arkankeli Racecourse is a horse racing venue in Arkankeli, Eastern [[karjania]]. It is the largest horse racing track by capacity in Karjania that only hosts Thoroughbred races, also known as flat races. It was constructed in 1907, with the first race being held on the 28th of November. The most prestigious event is the G1 [[thousand-lakes-derby]], a race famed across [[evria]].

## History

As flat racing was growing more and more popular in Karjania in the early 1900s, especially in the eastern regions, a group of nobles from Arkankeli founded the Arkankeli Thoroughbred Racing Association (ATRA) in 1902. ATRA later financed the construction of a new horse racing venue in Arkankeli in 1905, and construction was finished by 1907. The facility was inaugurated on the 28th of November,when the first running of the Arkankeli Stakes was held. The race featured only horses bred by the ATRA, and the first horse born outside Arkankeli to compete was the mare Suuret Korvat in 1912, who was born in [[pietari]]. Horses from outside of Karjania wouldn't compete until after [[great-war]].  In 1920, during the Karjanian Civil War, the Thousand Lakes Derby was first held, as flat racing began to surpass harness racing in popularity east of Pietari. After the Civil War in 1921, the 1921 Thousand Lakes Derby, won by Karjanian racehorse Vahvat Jalat, who also won the 1920 edition, marked the first appearance of international racehorses at the Arkankeli Racecourse. Next year in 1922, the Livonian stallion Vėjo Spyris became the first international winner. The 1923 edition was won by the Elvesterian Tumbling Winds, who became the first mare to win the Thousand Lakes Derby. It has been held every year since 1920 without exception, the majority won by Karjanian horses. Arkankeli Racecourse received a large expansion in 1928, with the edition of around 7,000 more standing and around 500 more sitting viewing opportunities, bringing it's full capacity up to the current 23,000. Alongside this, the now famous Whaler's Hotel was built, able to accommodate around one thousand people in it's 3 buildings. The construction of the Racecourse brought the city of Arkankeli a rise in tourism.

## Facilities

The Arkankeli Racecourse is located on the outskirts of the city of Arkankeli, about 39 km away from the inner city centre. When it was constructed in 1907, it was 2 Km away from the nearest residential area, and about 300 meters away from the closest building, the Arkankeli Rubber Factory. Today, there are several houses and streets right next to the racecourse, with about 700 people permanently living within a kilometre of it. The complex employs around 2,000 permanent workers between the racecourse, hotel and the Arkankeli Horse Stud, located around 400 meters from the track. The main grandstand, overlooking the finish line has a capacity of up to 18,000, while the full facility can host around 23,000 spectators. The track itself is a simple, oval shaped racecourse, with a full length of 2500 meters, although every race except the Thousand Lakes Derby is run on just 1200m. The Arkankeli Racecourse only hosts dirt surface races. It has held over 200 events throughout it's over 25 year long history.
